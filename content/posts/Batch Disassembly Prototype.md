There are plenty of reason that Remanufacturing is certainly not the reverse of Manufacturing. One of the key aspects is the question of High Variety / Low volume operations given the necessity of suitable take-loops. Another the question of adaptive processing given the largely varying End of Life Conditions. 

Within in the Remanufactory project, we're therefore working on advancing Batch-Disassembly processes, i.e., adaptive processes that can adjust to both the variety/variants of the collected cores (often not more than presorted after brand), as well as adaptive operations including vision, data extraction, etc.
